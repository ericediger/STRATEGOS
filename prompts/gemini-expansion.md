## Report Requirements

- Cover every entity in depth. The primary target should receive the most thorough treatment, competitors comparably, and secondary players proportionally to the evidence available.
- Include specific figures, dates, names, and data points throughout. Revenue numbers, funding amounts, headcounts, pricing tiers, customer counts, market share percentages, and direct quotes from sources.
- Cite sources inline where you found specific data.
- When sources present conflicting information, document both positions rather than choosing one.
- Investigate and report on negative signals actively, not just positive narrative.
- Structure findings with clear headings per entity and topic area.
- Do not summarize or compress. Include everything relevant you found. This research feeds a downstream strategic analysis agent that needs the most thorough, evidence-rich raw material possible.
- The most common failure mode is producing a high-level overview instead of deep, detailed research. If you found substantial evidence on a topic, include all of it.

Do not use em-dashes. Use commas, semicolons, or separate sentences instead.
